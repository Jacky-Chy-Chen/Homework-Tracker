# Materials extension

Mode: Operate. Preserve the existing Files page, teacher uploads, subject groups, theme and navigation. The approved Phase 5 plan settles the task and permissions.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Separate trusted teacher resources from immediate student contributions without making students leave Files.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit the existing Plus Jakarta Sans, neutral surfaces, accent controls, subject swatches, row lists and light/dark tokens. No new branding or decorative assets.

STORY: Choose Teacher Materials or Student Notes; browse by subject; signed-in students contribute, manage their own files, and privately report a problem. Only admins see the report queue.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Keep the Files heading, then a two-section navigation row. Student Notes starts with its verification disclaimer and an Upload notes action; rows put title, contributor, date and review status before file and ownership controls. Forms expand in place.

FORM: Narrow established-surface extension; no concept seed applies. Inline editing and reporting retain browsing context and keyboard labels.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
